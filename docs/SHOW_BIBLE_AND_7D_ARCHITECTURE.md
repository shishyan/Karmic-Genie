# *Karmic Genie Show* — Show Bible & 7D Architectural Specification

## 1. Narrative Philosophy

1. **Seven Hats, One Shared Human Table:**
   Every ~7-minute widescreen episode (`~07:05–07:15`) steps into **seven distinct roles, ages, and global geographies** affected by a pressing modern shift—intercutting stadium-forum voices with quiet, candid real-life vignettes from homes, kitchens, parks, and late-night offices.
2. **Verified Real-World Metrics Only:**
   Every figure shown on the studio's curved glass barriers comes from verifiable primary filings and industry trackers—never invented numbers.
3. **Empathetic, Non-Preachy Host (`@Me`):**
   The Host listens, holds unhurried silence, and connects lived human realities without lecturing or cross-examining guests.
4. **Signature Show Punchline (End of Act I):**
   When human arguments and spreadsheet math hit a wall, the Host turns toward the dormant jeweled lamp on the center glass table and asks:
   > ***"What does Genie say — the 7D way?"***
5. **The Karmic Genie as a Peaceful, Polite Wise Saint (Act II):**
   * Rises **cross-legged** in emerald, warm gold, soft magenta, and sapphire stardust above the center glass table.
   * Speaks in a **soft, serene, courteous, unhurried voice**—never booming, harsh, or scolding.
   * References timeless wisdom only by brief essence (leaving curious viewers to read the full story at home) rather than preaching.
   * **100% Open-Ended Paths:** Never hands out rigid prescriptions or commands that trigger ego defense; offers gentle, open-ended questions along *The 7D Way* so every viewer's heart discovers its own missing step.
