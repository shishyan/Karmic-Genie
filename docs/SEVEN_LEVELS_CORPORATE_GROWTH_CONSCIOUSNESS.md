## 2. Definitions of the 7 Developmental Stages

1. **Stage 1: Seed Inception & Germination (*Vithu* - வித்து):** The founding spark, solving a real human need, initial sacrifice and ideation.
2. **Stage 2: Root Survival & Metabolism (*Ver* - வேர்):** Achieving cash-flow positive solvency, revenue generation, financial self-defense. (Basal metabolic floor—necessary, but NOT the purpose).
3. **Stage 3: Vegetative Trunk & Operational Scale (*Thandu* - தண்டு):** Building reliable organizational pipes, global distribution, high-uptime infrastructure, enterprise stability.
4. **Stage 4: Foliage & Leaf Photosynthesis (*Ilai* - இலை):** Developer ecosystem delight, customer warmth, ingesting societal waste and turning it into healthy community atmosphere.
5. **Stage 5: Flowering & Pollination (*Malar* - மலர்):** Pure innovation, aesthetic grace, elevating human dignity, joyful work cultures, inspiring the world's imagination.
6. **Stage 6: Fruit-Yielding Universal Nourishment (*Kani* - கனி):** Providing baseline life-support goods, public utilities, and affordable or free life-saving nutrition/health/energy to all of humanity.
7. **Stage 7: Seed-Sharing & Liberation (*Viduthalai / Moksha* - விடுதலை):** Releasing unencumbered open-source primitives, open-weight models, irrevocable public trusts (e.g., Patagonia), enabling the whole ecosystem to outlive the corporation.
